## Round 06 (2026-10-21)

- **R06-01 — UX-020 `stock` trusted agent-written data.** `Offer.inventoryLevel` is written by the agent, so it can't be the oracle. → Stock-count phrases are compared only with an Operator-owned source mapped in the new `ux.stockSources` (`dataset` field, merchant-feed column, or seeded-database query); a phrase on an unmapped page is a facts-oracle `forbidden-value` finding with or without an `Offer`. · `spec/packs/ux.md` UX-020 + policy block; `spec/04-algorithms.md` § 1.2.
- **R06-02 — PRV-025 escaped as `notConfigured`.** A product page with no matching manufacturer gave a warning and could PASS. → New 04 § 1.2 *required-facts rules*: missing legally required Operator facts are blocking `missing-element` findings owned by `operator` (handoff, never PASS). PRV-025 no-match uses it. · `spec/packs/privacy.md` PRV-025; `spec/04-algorithms.md` § 1.2.
- **R06-03 — No rule against hidden or AI-targeted text.** → New AEO-014 with parts `hidden-text` (P1), `ai-addressed` (P0) and `agent-parity` (P0: Bingbot and AI search/user-fetch agents vs Chrome). New ruleset `ai-addressed-phrases.json`. Ownership: WP-412, browser channel WP-608. · `spec/packs/answer-engines.md`; `BUILD_MAP.md` WP-104/412/608; `trace/ownership.tsv`.
- **R06-04 — Asian-market seller identity notices missing.** → Jurisdictions `kr-ecommerce`, `jp-sct`, `cn-icp`; PRV-022 parts of the same names; `business.json` `legal.mailOrderNumber`, `icpNumber`, `psbNumber`; `legalPages.commercialTransactions`; ruleset `sct-labels`. Missing values are required-facts findings. · `spec/packs/privacy.md`; `spec/03-formats.md` § 14; `BUILD_MAP.md` WP-104.
- **R06-05 — Ownership was prose-only and incomplete.** 19 `TR-*` requirements had no owner; 40 rules were split across WPs without named slices; range/slash notation hid owners. → `trace/ownership.tsv` is the single source of ownership at rule/part/channel level, enforced by `tools/trace/spec-lint.mjs`. · `BUILD_MAP.md` § 4.1, § 5.
- **R06-06 — WPs built before the machinery they need.** → Dependencies added: WP-409 (+407, 408), WP-413 (+409), WP-504 (+303, 407), WP-701 (+602), WP-702 (+409, 705, 207), WP-703 (+414), WP-801 (+701, 412). AGT-005, AEO-012 and CQ-006 move to WP-801 (they read `_main/routes.jsonl`). · `BUILD_MAP.md` § 5; `trace/ownership.tsv`.
- **R06-07 — API-004 used the `baseline` channel for a static comparison with released contracts.** → Channel is `contract` (G2). · `spec/packs/data-api.md`.
- **R06-08 — 41 spec sections cited by no WP** (report format, MCP, licensing, versioning, debt, baselines, …). → Section-ownership table in BUILD_MAP § 5; spec-lint fails an uncited section. · `BUILD_MAP.md` § 5.
- **R06-09 — Nothing stopped a builder from skipping specified work.** → Completeness contract BUILD_MAP § 4.1: generated WP cards, fixtures by a separate author and locked, reviewer holdouts, evidence from the built binary and a fresh corpus run on the committed tree, `trace-check` as the done gate, deferred slices keep a WP open, stub scan, mutation testing at phase ends, Stop hook on false "done". WP-000, WP-209 and DoD item 1 updated. · `BUILD_MAP.md` § 2, § 4.1, WP-000, WP-209, § 7.
- **R06-10 — Concreteness audit (404 findings: 22 Blocker, 268 Major, 109 Minor, + 5 carried-over should-adds).** Every finding fixed (313) or adapted (91) by one fixer per file group in two passes; per-finding outcomes, spec locations and reasons are in `audit/rounds/06/resolution.md` and `resolution-*-{A,B}.md`. New rules: CQ-010 (copied third-party text), UX-022 (launch conversion confirmation), REF-014 (re-platforming image URLs), SEC-067 (email ownership before trust); new jurisdiction `uk-consumer`; shared 04 § 15 *hidden content* and *disclosure exemption*, 04 § 11 generic skeleton hash, 04 § 12 token overlap, lock-digest PRNG; DECISIONS D-001..D-004 applied. · all spec files; `BUILD_MAP.md` WP-104; `trace/ownership.tsv`.

- **R09-02 — Stage 0 closed; build-first, fixture-first.** The Operator closed the audit loop (DECISIONS D-006). `CLAUDE.md`, `ORCHESTRATOR_PROMPT.md` and `audit/README.md` now say so; fixture authors list undecidable cases in `wp/WP-<id>/spec-gaps.md`, which are fixed in the spec before the WP continues; one escape-path audit per phase; explicit model routing for the build. · `CLAUDE.md`, `ORCHESTRATOR_PROMPT.md`, `audit/README.md`.

- **R09-03 — BUILD_MAP contradictions found while building WP-000.** (a) § 4.1 item 7 stub-scan banned the words `TODO`/`FIXME` anywhere in `crates/` and `rules/`, but the MET-008 leak scan (04 § 12) must list them as data → markers are matched only inside comments; `todo!`/`unimplemented!` calls stay banned. (b) WP-000 put an absolute short `target-dir` in the committed `.cargo/config.toml`, which breaks CI on other OSes → it lives in a machine-local Cargo config in the repo's parent directory. · `BUILD_MAP.md` § 4.1 item 7, WP-000.
- **R09-04 — Ruleset data files can't carry provenance keys.** BUILD_MAP WP-104 acceptance required `source`/`retrieved` fields in every ruleset file, and 03 § 1 required `formatVersion` in every JSON object file; most ruleset files are locale-keyed objects (`{ "<locale>": [...] }`), where extra keys would read as locales. → ruleset data under `rules/**` is versioned by the ruleset id and exempt from `formatVersion` (03 § 1); provenance (source URLs, retrieval date, licence, attribution) lives in `rules/provenance.json`, one entry per file. · `spec/03-formats.md` § 1, `BUILD_MAP.md` § 5 acceptance table.
- **R09-05 — Acceptance tests had no owner.** § 4.1 kept fixtures away from builders but not the `wp_*` acceptance tests, so a builder could write or weaken the test that judges it. → the orchestrator writes them on `main` before dispatch; merge-gate refuses `tests/wp_*.rs` changes (self-test 10/10, watched-fail without the rule: 9/10); CI's Rust job excludes `wp_*` tests and the trace-gates job judges them for claimed WPs. · `BUILD_MAP.md` § 4.1 item 3, `ORCHESTRATOR_PROMPT.md` loop step 1, `tools/trace/merge-gate.mjs`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.
