# Pack: web-delivery

> Everything search engines and AI crawlers need from a site: rendering, HTTP semantics, URL identity, metadata, structured data, internal and outbound links, indexation and feeds, production launch checks, localisation, mobile parity, crawler policy, trust pages and content hygiene (placeholders, spelling, bulk additions). Applies to `public` surfaces; status/robots rules also cover `app` and `api`.

Local business facts (name, phones, addresses, hours, locations) come from `quality/data/business.json` (03 § 14), not from policy.

**Locale segment.** The URL segment of a locale is its BCP 47 tag in the case of the service `pathCase` (`lower`: `en-US` → `/en-us/`; `insensitive`, `preserve`: the tag as written in `locales`), unless `localePaths` sets it. **Page locale** (and "URL locale", the same thing): 04 § 15 *Page locale*, which reads this segment and also defines the per-locale list lookup and the outcome for a locale with no list entry. With `localePrefix: "always"`, a URL whose first path segment after the service `mount` is no locale segment takes `defaultLocale` for list lookups (04 § 15) and is still a MET-007 finding.

Validation:
- **WCF-001** · P0 · cfg — Every service with a `public` surface has an https `origin` without path, query or port.
- **WCF-002** · P0 · cfg — `defaultLocale ∈ locales`; `localePrefix: "never"` ⇒ exactly one locale; `xDefault` null or a declared path; every `localePaths` key is in `locales`, every value matches `^[a-z0-9-]+$`, and the segments of all locales (overrides and derived) are pairwise distinct.
- **WCF-003** · P0 · cfg — Service `trailingSlash` equals adapter `config` setting `trailingSlash`, service `mount` equals setting `basePath`, `locales`/`defaultLocale` equal settings `i18n.locales`/`i18n.defaultLocale` (compared as sets of BCP 47 tags, case-insensitive). A setting the adapter's `config` readers do not return is not compared; an adapter with an empty `config` → `noAdapterSupport`. Failure class `value-mismatch` (key, policy value, framework value).
- **WCF-004** · P0 · cfg — `warnMin < warnMax ≤ hardMax` for title and description lengths (positive integers). Value ranges: `parity.minOverlap` and `parity.mobileMinOverlap` in (0, 1]; `maxClickDepth` an integer 1–20; `sitemap.maxUrlsPerFile` an integer 1–50,000; `externalLinks.maxUrls` an integer ≥ 1; `timeZone` an IANA zone known to the toolchain tz database; `locales` valid BCP 47 tags; `ogLocales` keys in `locales`, values matching `^[a-z]{2,3}_[A-Z]{2}$`; `xDefault` null or a wildcard-free path pattern equal to the homepage path of a locale; `trackingParams` entries are parameter-name globs in which `*` is the only wildcard, matched case-sensitively; `llmsTxt` one of `required`, `optional`, `off`; `leakIgnore` entries parse as CSS selectors (Selectors Level 4, no pseudo-elements); `reviewSources[].pattern` parses (03 § 2.1) and `reviewSources[].ratingsSource` equals the `source` of a `business.json` `ratings[]` entry. Failure class `value-mismatch` (key, value).
- **WCF-005** · P1 · cfg — Every `targets[].url` resolves to a declared or discovered route.
- **WCF-006** · P0 · cfg — All patterns parse (03 § 2.1); `indexRules` have no specificity ties, counting `staticPaths[]` entries and `privacy.legalPages` values as exact-path entries with their `index` value (two entries of equal specificity with different `index` values are a tie).
- **WCF-007** · P0 · cfg — `syndicated[].originalUrl` is absolute https on a host that is no service origin, using only `{params}` of its pattern; `aliasHosts` are concrete or `*.`-prefixed hostnames disjoint from service origin hosts; `trustPages` values are declared or discovered public routes; `content.maxNewPagesPerChange ≥ 1`; `aiCrawlers` keys not in the pinned `ai-agents.json` are W (unknown token).
- **WCF-008** · P0 · cfg — `docs.versions`, when set: `latest` is a path without wildcards or params; every `older` entry parses as a pattern whose literal prefix (the text before its first `*` or `{`) is non-empty, ends in `/`, and is not a prefix of `latest` or of another `older` prefix (the version root).

## 2. Definitions and SEO coverage map

**Indexable page**: 04 § 15 *Indexable page* (the only definition; "indexable canonical" and "indexable URL" mean the same set). Nothing on the page (a `noindex`, a robots.txt disallow, a canonical elsewhere) removes a URL from the set; on an indexable page those are findings of MET-006, IDX-004 and URL-004. The query parameters an indexable page may carry (its item 5) are the ones URL-001 admits.

**HTTP sample**, **browser-sampled pages**: 04 § 10. In this pack "public pages" without another qualifier means the pages of the HTTP sample.

**Visible text** (every rule of this pack that says "visible"). Raw HTML (channel `html`): the text nodes of `<body>` that are not inside `<script>`, `<style>`, `<template>` or `<noscript>`, and not inside an element with the `hidden` attribute, `aria-hidden="true"`, or a `style` attribute setting `display:none`, `visibility:hidden`, `font-size:0` or `opacity:0` — except an element that is a disclosure candidate by its markup (04 § 15 *Disclosure exemption* items 1–4), whose text counts as visible. Browser-sampled pages (channel `browser`): the text of elements that are not hidden under 04 § 15 *Hidden content*, with the verified *Disclosure exemption* applied. SCH-006, SCH-010 and TRS-002 carry channel `browser` for this reason: on a browser-sampled page, a value found in the raw-HTML visible text whose element is hidden in the browser is a finding (`value-mismatch`: element, the hidden-content item that holds).

**Data file shapes** (ruleset files of this pack; WP-104). `placeholders.json`: `{ "<primary subtag>|*": { lorem: [string], starter: [string], faviconSha256: [hex], phoneRanges: [{ from, to }] (E.164), addresses: [string], domains: [host pattern], names: [string], imageHosts: [host] } }` (`*` applies to every language). `generic-anchors.json`, `generic-titles.json`, `stop-words.json`, `evergreen-words.json`, `comparison-patterns.json`, `load-more-labels.json`: `{ "<primary subtag>": [string] }`, compared whole-word after NFKC and case folding. Every per-locale list of this pack (these files, `placeholders.json`, the soft-404 phrases, CLDR data) is looked up by page locale, and a locale with no entry is handled, as 04 § 15 *Page locale* says. `scaffold-routes.json`, `default-documents.json`, `placeholder-handles.json`: `[string]`. `tracking-param-expansions.json`: `{ "<trackingParams glob>": "<parameter name>" }`. `content-signals.json`: `{ directive: "Content-Signal", signals: ["search", "ai-input", "ai-train"], values: ["yes", "no"] }`. `social-agents.json`: `[{ token, userAgent }]`. `search-agents.json`: `[{ token, variant: "desktop"|"smartphone", userAgent }]`. `ai-agents.json`: `[{ token, vendor, category: "training"|"search"|"user-fetch", userAgent, honoursRobots: bool, docUrl }]` (`userAgent` is the desktop string; a `W.X.Y.Z` browser-version placeholder in it matches `\d+\.\d+\.\d+\.\d+` and is sent as `0.0.0.0`). `schemaorg.json`: `{ version, types: { "<Type>": { supertypes: [string], properties: { "<prop>": { ranges: [string] } } } }, enumerations: { "<Enum>": [member URL] } }`.

## 3. Rendering & HTTP — `RTE-*`

- **RTE-001** · P0 · html, browser — Content in the initial HTML, on browser-sampled public pages (04 § 10), raw HTML vs rendered DOM. The rendered DOM is `document.documentElement.outerHTML` in the `desktop` viewport (03 § 10), captured 2 s after `load` with no scrolling or interaction (the same moment as 04 § 15 *Hidden content*). Part `head` — each of `<title>`, meta description, `link[rel=canonical]`, meta robots, `<h1>` and every `script[type="application/ld+json"]` present in the rendered DOM is present in the raw HTML with an equal value (text NFKC, whitespace-collapsed; attribute values exact; JSON-LD compared as the set of blocks after JCS canonicalisation); part `links` — every same-origin `a[href]` target (canonicalised, 04 § 10 normalisation) in the rendered DOM whose canonical is indexable is also an `a[href]` target in the raw HTML; part `main` — `<main>` text token overlap (04 § 12; reference = rendered `<main>`) ≥ `parity.minOverlap` (Chinese-locale pages: SE-501 owns this comparison with threshold 1.0, and part `main` does not apply to them). Elements missing from both raw and rendered HTML are judged by their own rules (MET-001, MET-002, URL-004, MET-006, MET-003, SCH-001), not here. Failure classes `missing-element` (with the element and URL), `value-mismatch` (raw vs rendered value), `similarity` (overlap value).
- **RTE-002** · P1 · ast — Hint for RTE-001: each public route file is matched against the adapter's `renderModes` (03 § 6; entries tried in order, the first match decides, adapters end the list with a catch-all entry for the framework default); a match with mode `csr` is a finding (`pattern-match`, route file and matched pattern). Adapters without `renderModes` → `noAdapterSupport`.
- **RTE-003** · W · ast — Tombstone (deprecated, rule metadata `lifecycle: deprecated`): client-side fetching of primary content is proven by RTE-001 part `main` and hinted by RTE-002; there is no separate detector.
- **RTE-004** · P0† · http — Status contract:

  | Case | Status | Robots | Canonical | Sitemap |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Indexable public page | 200 | index,follow (or none) | self | yes |
  | Public page, `noindex` by `indexRules` | 200 | noindex,follow | self | no |
  | `app` page, authenticated | 200 | noindex,nofollow | — | no |
  | `app` page, anonymous | 302/303 → `auth.loginPath` (or 401) | noindex | — | no |
  | Unknown URL | 404 | noindex | none | no |
  | Retired path, no successor | 410 | noindex | none | no |
  | Moved path / non-canonical variant (case, slash, `?page=1`) | `redirectStatus`, one hop | — | — | no |
  | Tracking or unknown query params | 200 | as page | clean URL | no |
  | Internal site-search results (IDX-012) | 200 | noindex (or path robots-disallowed) | none | no |
  | Empty / out-of-range paginated page | 404 | noindex | none | no |
  | Dependency failure | 5xx | — | — | — |
  | Non-production deploy (`DEPLOY_ENV` ≠ production) | as above | noindex,nofollow + `X-Robots-Tag` | self | no sitemap |

  The table is the contract; RTE-004 judges only its **Status** column. Parts: `status` (P0†) — the response status (and, for redirect rows, that a `Location` is sent) equals the row's Status; in the preview pass (04 § 2 item 4) the `preview-20` sample must answer the Status column too (its Robots column is IDX-005); `soft-404` (P0†) — every 200 `text/html` response of the HTTP sample and of every probe below is tested with the 04 § 12 soft-404 detection, using for the page language the union of the ruleset list and `soft404Phrases[<primary subtag>]`; a match is a finding (`status-mismatch`: URL, the matched phrase or `near-empty`). The other columns are owned elsewhere and evaluated only there: Robots → MET-006 (IDX-006 for the 404/410 rows); Canonical → URL-004 (IDX-006 for 404/410); Sitemap → IDX-001/IDX-007; moved and non-canonical rows → URL-002/URL-008 (redirect target, hop count); tracking params → URL-005; site search → IDX-012; pagination → URL-006; dependency failure → RTE-007; non-production → IDX-005. Probes per service: Unknown URL = `/sg-{n}` (the 04 § 15 fallback-body path, no extension; `{n}` = the first 8 hex characters of SHA-256(lock digest ‖ `0x00` ‖ `sg-probe`), the same `{n}` in every probe that uses it) and `/{p}/sg-{n}` for each distinct literal first path segment `{p}` of `staticPaths` and adapter route patterns; Retired = every `retired` entry without `successor`; Empty pagination = for each series of URL-006, the page number one past its last page; `app` anonymous = every `app`-surface route seed requested without the session cookie; `app` authenticated = the same seeds with the test account's session (03 § 4). Failure class `status-mismatch` (URL, expected and actual status).
- **RTE-005** · P0 · ast, http — The 404 decision happens before the response is committed. ast: the adapter traps whose `guards` include RTE-005 (02 § 5, 03 § 5; e.g. Next.js `notFound()` after streaming starts) — a match is `pattern-match` (route file, trap). http: for each route flagged by such a trap, the RTE-006 probe URL of that route answers 404 in the status line (`status-mismatch`). RTE-006 does not report a URL that RTE-005 has reported.
- **RTE-006** · P0 · ast, http — Closed route space: unknown **route parameters** are rejected or validated before render. Probes: for each dynamic route and each of its params in turn, the URL with that param set to `sg-probe-404` (a param with a `:regex` constraint gets the shortest string matching the regex that is no inventory or fixture value, ties broken by the lock-digest PRNG, 04 § 15; no such string exists → that param is `skipped(closed-set)`) and every other param set to a value that answered 200 in the crawl (none → the route is `skipped(no-valid-sample)`) returns 404 (`status-mismatch`: URL, param, actual status).
- **RTE-007** · P0† · http — Dependency errors return 5xx (stale caches may keep serving), never 404 or a 200 shell; proven by the core fault-injection facility in G4 (04 § 4.5: database stopped, upstream refused) on the `fault-20` sample of `public` and `app` surfaces (`remote: write`: fault injection, 04 § 15). Owner of this behaviour for pages; `api`-surface routes are RES-001, and routes matching `resilience.degradable` for the faulted dependency are judged by RES-003 (200 allowed there).
- **RTE-008** · P1 · html — Duplicate head tags in the raw HTML of each public page: more than one `<meta name="description">`, more than one `<meta name="viewport">`, or more than one `<meta name="robots">` (`googlebot` and `bingbot` metas are other names) is a `duplicate-element` finding (tag, count). Duplicate `<title>` (MET-001) and `link[rel=canonical]` (URL-004) are judged only there; duplicates added by scripts surface through RTE-001 `head`.
- **RTE-009** · P0 · html, http — On each indexable page of the HTTP sample: no `<meta http-equiv="refresh">` in the raw HTML (any `content`, `<noscript>` included; `http-equiv` compared case-insensitively) and no `Refresh` response header (`forbidden-value`: element or header, URL).
- **RTE-010** · P0† · http, ast — No cloaking. User agents come from the pinned ruleset `search-agents.json` (§ 2 shape, for Googlebot, Bingbot, YandexBot, Yeti, Baiduspider, SeznamBot, Applebot and Chrome; SE-002 and SE-008 use the engine tokens). Part `behaviour` (P0†, http): each indexable page in the HTTP sample (04 § 10) is fetched, per variant, twice with the Chrome UA and once with the Googlebot UA of the same variant, without following redirects (a UA-dependent HTTP redirect is a status or `Location` difference judged here; JavaScript and meta redirects are SE-122). Only when the Operator's `search-engines.features` contains `paywalled-content`, elements matched by a class-selector `hasPart.cssSelector` of `isAccessibleForFree: false` markup (SE-109) are removed from all three responses first; without that feature nothing is removed. Then the Googlebot response has the same status, `Location` and content type as the first Chrome response, an equal generic `<main>` skeleton hash (04 § 11), and identical `<title>`, meta description, robots directives (meta and `X-Robots-Tag`), canonical, hreflang pairs and JSON-LD `@type` and property-name sets, compared as in RTE-014. When the two Chrome responses have identical normalised `<main>` text (NFKC, whitespace-collapsed), the Googlebot text must be identical too; otherwise only the skeleton is compared. Part `empty-ua` (W, http): the same comparison for a request without a `User-Agent` header against the desktop Chrome response (bot protection legitimately blocks empty user agents). Part `source-hint` (W, ast): 04 § 13. Failure classes `skeleton-mismatch` (first differing node path), `status-mismatch`, `value-mismatch` (text, or the differing head field with both values). Bingbot and AI agents vs Chrome: AEO-014 `agent-parity`.
- **RTE-011** · P1 · ast — In files of role route, layout or component (04 § 7.1): part `clock` — a call to an adapter `clockApis` entry, and part `random` — a call to an adapter `randomApis` entry (03 § 6; e.g. `Math.random`, `crypto.randomUUID`, Python `random.*`), whose result flows (04 § 13 data flow) into a text node, attribute value, JSON-LD value or response header, except the 04 § 13 RTE-011 allowlist (`pattern-match`: call, sink). An adapter with no `clockApis` (resp. `randomApis`) → `noAdapterSupport` for that part. Locale and time-zone arguments of formatters are I18-006.
- **RTE-012** · P1 · build — Two builds from identical inputs (clean output directory, same lock digest) produce identical public HTML (`--profile nightly`): each build is started and the decoded response bodies of the `preview-20` sample (04 § 10; URLs chosen from the first build's crawl and requested again from the second) are compared, after replacing in both bodies with `⟦asset⟧`: every URL listed in the adapter `bundleManifest`; every path segment or file-name stem matching `[A-Za-z0-9_-]{8,}` that differs between the two builds at the same DOM position; every `integrity` and `nonce` attribute value. The report shows the first differing URL and byte offset (`value-mismatch`).
- **RTE-013** · P1 · browser — Browser-sampled public pages (04 § 10) and flow steps on public surfaces. Part `uncaught` — zero uncaught errors from origin scripts; part `hydration` — zero console messages matching the adapter `hydrationErrorPatterns` (03 § 6; none declared → this part `noAdapterSupport`); part `subresource` — zero failed (4xx/5xx) same-origin subresources. Errors from non-origin scripts are excluded (04 § 6.1). Failure class `runtime-error` (message, script URL, page). Owns these checks on public surfaces; TST-009 on app surfaces.
- **RTE-014** · P0 · html, http — Mobile parity between the smartphone and desktop Googlebot UAs (`search-agents.json`; Google indexes the smartphone response), checked on every indexable page in the HTTP sample (04 § 10): raw-HTML `<main>` text and links token overlap (04 § 12; reference = the desktop response, so desktop-only content lowers it) ≥ `parity.mobileMinOverlap`; identical `<title>`, meta description, robots directives (meta and `X-Robots-Tag`), canonical, hreflang set (`hreflang` → `href` pairs); structured data compared as the set of JSON-LD `@type`s plus, for each node (matched by `@id`, else by `@type` and document position), its set of property names. The overlap threshold is the only text test. Failure classes `similarity`, `value-mismatch`.
- **RTE-015** · P1 · http — Fetch-size limit (Googlebot reads only the first 2 MB of an HTML file, and of each referenced JS/CSS file, uncompressed): part `size` — the decoded body of an indexable page is ≤ 2,000,000 bytes (part `size-warn`, W: > 1,000,000 bytes); part `cutoff` — the end of `</head>` (with title, meta robots, description, canonical, hreflang), the first `<h1>`, every JSON-LD block and `</main>` lie within the first 2,000,000 decoded bytes; part `subresource` — each same-origin `<script src>`, `<link rel="stylesheet">` and `<link rel="modulepreload">` referenced in the raw HTML of an indexable page has a decoded body ≤ 2,000,000 bytes.
- **RTE-016** · P0 (part `invalid-element` P1, part `noscript-body` W) · html, browser — Head integrity on the indexable pages of the HTTP sample (Google ignores every head element after an invalid one and treats the head as ended there). **Head region:** in the raw HTML response to the Googlebot smartphone user agent (`search-agents.json`), the token stream from the first `<head>` start tag (absent: the first token after the doctype and `<html>` start tag) up to the first `</head>` end tag or `<body>` start tag, tokenised with the html5ever tokenizer without tree construction; the content of `<script>`, `<style>`, `<template>` and `<noscript>` elements is not inspected as head tokens. **Allowed head elements:** `title`, `meta`, `link`, `script`, `style`, `base`, `noscript`, `template`. **Signal elements** (HTML namespace only; an SVG `<title>` is none): `title`; `meta` with `name` `robots`, `googlebot`, `bingbot`, `description` or `viewport`, `meta[http-equiv="refresh" i]`, `meta[charset]`; `link` with `rel` `canonical`, or `rel="alternate"` with `hreflang`; `script[type="application/ld+json"]`. Parts `invalid-element`, `signals-after`, `body-signal`, `noscript-body`:
  - `invalid-element` (P1, html) — a start tag in the head region that is not an allowed head element, or a non-whitespace character token outside those elements (reported as `#text`), is a finding (`pattern-match`: element, byte offset, URL);
  - `signals-after` (P0, html) — when the head region holds an invalid element and ≥ 1 signal element follows it (before `</head>`), each such signal element is a finding (`forbidden-value`: signal element, the invalid element before it, URL), and `invalid-element` is not reported for that page;
  - `body-signal` (P0, html, browser) — in the DOM built from the raw HTML (html) and in the rendered DOM of browser-sampled pages captured as in RTE-001 (browser), no signal element other than `script[type="application/ld+json"]` and `meta[name=viewport]` is a descendant of `<body>` (`forbidden-value`: element, `raw` or `rendered`; rendered-only findings name the inserting script when the browser records it); an element already reported by `signals-after` is not reported again;
  - `noscript-body` (W, html) — a `<noscript>` in the head region with a child element other than `link`, `style` or `meta` (WHATWG HTML, "in head noscript" insertion mode: browsers with scripting disabled end the head there).

  A response that is not `text/html` → `notApplicable`. No policy keys.
- **RTE-017** · P1 (part `button` W) · browser, html — No content that exists only after a click. On browser-sampled public pages (04 § 10), for every control inside `<main>` whose 04 § 15 *Disclosure exemption* (items 1–3) was verified by the activation that section performs, C is the content it reveals (the element referenced by `aria-controls`, or the `<details>` body). Words are UAX #29 words after NFKC and case folding. Part `empty` (browser): C had 0 words before activation and ≥ 20 after it (`missing-element`: control accessible name, C's selector, word counts before and after, URL). Part `absent-in-raw` (html, browser): C has ≥ 20 words after activation and the token overlap (04 § 12; reference = the activated text of C) with the raw-HTML text of `<body>` (text nodes outside `<script>`, `<style>`, `<template>`) is < 0.9 (`similarity`: overlap value, C's selector). Part `button` (browser): a control whose accessible name matches ruleset `load-more-labels.json` for the page locale and whose click appends ≥ 5 `a[href]` to indexable pages absent from the raw HTML, on a page with no plain `<a href>` to the next page of a URL-006 series (URL-006 part `links` owns series), is a finding (`missing-element`: control, the first 3 added URLs). Exempt: dialogs and popovers (item 4), carousel slides (item 5; RTE-001 `links` judges their links), controls on `app` surfaces, and controls whose activation navigates to another URL (navigation is LNK-001). No browser in the run → `skipped(no-browser)`; no verified disclosure on a page → `notApplicable` for that page.

## 4. URL identity — `URL-*`

- **URL-001** · P0 · cfg, http — Canonical form (the one definition, used by URL-002, URL-004, URL-009 and URL-014): scheme https; host = the service origin host; path under the service `mount`; path case per the service `pathCase` (03 § 3.2): `lower` → the path has no uppercase letter and any other casing is a non-canonical variant (URL-002); `insensitive` → the case the route emits, other casings answer 200 with that canonical (URL-004); `preserve` → the case the route emits, other casings are different paths; trailing slash per the service `trailingSlash` (root `/` excepted); locale segment per `localePrefix` and § 1 *Locale segment*; no fragment; query only the parameters an indexable page may carry (04 § 15 *Indexable page* item 5: declared `pagination` and `self-canonical` `contentParams`, `search-engines.products.variantParams` on variant pages, the parameter sets of `search-engines.facets.indexable`), sorted (04 § 10 normalisation). Checked on every `link[rel=canonical]` and hreflang `href` of the HTTP sample and every sitemap `<loc>`: a URL not in canonical form is `value-mismatch` (URL, violated component: `scheme`, `host`, `case`, `slash`, `locale`, `query`). Internal `a[href]` targets are LNK-002.
- **URL-002** · P0 · http, edge — Non-canonical variants reach the canonical in exactly one hop with `redirectStatus` (auth redirects exempt); no chains or loops; a canonical URL returns 200. Probes for each indexable page of the HTTP sample: (1) the opposite trailing-slash form (not for `/`); (2) with `pathCase: lower`, the path upper-cased; (3) for a URL-006 series, page 1 requested with `?{param}=1` (style `query`) or `/1` (style `path`). Each answers one hop to the canonical URL with status `redirectStatus` (`redirect-chain` with the hop list, `status-mismatch`, `redirect-target`). Further variants (default documents, doubled slashes, path parameters): URL-014. Edge-owned variants (http→https, apex↔www) are verified in G8.
- **URL-003** · P0 · ast — One URL module builds every emitted public URL from route params + the configured origin (data-flow rule, 04 § 13).
- **URL-004** · P0 · html — Applies to every 200 `text/html` public response of the HTTP sample and of the URL-005 probes. Exactly one absolute `<link rel="canonical">` equal to the expected canonical, never cross-locale. Expected canonical: a family or hub page → the oracle; any other indexable page, and a page with `indexRules` `index: false` → itself in canonical form (URL-001); a URL with tracking or unknown params, or a `canonical-clean` content param → the clean URL (URL-005); an internal site-search result (IDX-012) → no canonical element at all (the exactly-one test does not apply); 404/410 → IDX-006. A canonical to another host is allowed only on pages matching `syndicated` (URL-012); a canonical to another path on the same host only on older-version docs pages (URL-013) and on product-variant URLs canonical to their `ProductGroup` page under SE-111.
- **URL-005** · P0 · http, html — Query params: tracking and unknown params → 200 with a canonical equal to the clean URL and no `noindex` (no redirect). Probes per indexable page of the HTTP sample: each `trackingParams` entry without `*` as `?{name}=sg`; for an entry with `*`, the parameter name ruleset `tracking-param-expansions.json` gives for it (a glob not listed: `*` replaced by `source`); `?sg-unknown=1`. Content params: for each `contentParams` entry whose pattern matches the page, `?{param}=sg-value`: mode `canonical-clean` → canonical equals the clean URL; mode `self-canonical` → canonical equals the probed URL in canonical form (URL-001). Every probe answers 200 without redirect and without `noindex` (`value-mismatch`, `status-mismatch`). Facet combinations not declared indexable (search-engines `facets`) are either robots-disallowed or fragment-based (SE-132); robots-disallowed facet URLs use canonical-to-clean and never `noindex` (a blocked page's noindex is never read, IDX-004).
- **URL-006** · P0 · http, html, browser — Pagination: page 1 has no page param (`?page=1` → one-hop redirect, probed and judged by URL-002); page N > 1 is self-canonical, and its title and description each contain N as a whole word (UAX #29; digits of any numbering system converted to ASCII, as 04 § 11 parse-back) and differ from the title and description of every other page of the series. A **series** is the URLs of one `pagination` entry or programmatic hub, followed through `?{param}=N` (style `query`) or `/{N}` (style `path`) from N = 2 until the first 404, at most 50 pages (04 § 10 trap limit). An **item** is a same-origin canonicalised `a[href]` target inside `<main>` that is not present on every page of the series. Part `items` (series of ≥ 2 pages) — every page has ≥ 1 item and no item appears on two pages; part `links` — page N links to N+1 (when it exists) with a plain `<a href>` in raw HTML; part `infinite-scroll` (browser) — when scrolling page 1 to the bottom (`element.scrollIntoView()` on the last `<main>` child, no wheel or touch events, network idle ≤ 3 s, repeated up to 5 times) adds item links without a navigation, each added item is an item of some page of the series. Failure classes `missing-element`, `duplicate-element`, `status-mismatch`.
- **URL-007** · P1 · data, diff, ast, site — Slugs: lowercase, hyphenated, no reserved segments, stable. Part `format` (site, data): every percent-decoded value of a dynamic route param in a crawled indexable URL, and every inventory slug, is NFC, matches `^[\p{Ll}\p{Lo}\p{Nd}\p{M}]+(-[\p{Ll}\p{Lo}\p{Nd}\p{M}]+)*$` and is not equal, case-insensitively, to a `reservedSegments` entry (`value-mismatch`: URL or row, value); literal route segments are exempt. Part `stable` (data, diff): a family row (same family + `rowId`) present in the dataset at the diff base (04 § 5) whose slug changed lists the old slug in `previousSlugs` (URL-008 then redirects it); no git → `skipped(no-git)`. Part `recompute` (ast): route files don't call an adapter `slugApis` entry (03 § 6) at render time (slugs are stored, not recomputed). Generator NFKD → strip marks → lowercase → `&`→`and` → non-alphanumerics to `-` → collapse/trim; collisions get a stable disambiguator. Part `slug-quality` (W, site): the last non-empty path segment of an indexable URL, split on `-`, has > 6 words, or contains ≥ 3 consecutive stop words of the page locale (ruleset `stop-words.json`, as KW-002), or the path contains a date (`YYYY-MM[-DD]` or `YYYY/MM[/DD]`, year 1900–2099, valid month/day) on a page without `Article`/`BlogPosting`/`NewsArticle` JSON-LD.
- **URL-008** · P0 · http — Moved URLs redirect in one hop to their current canonical (moves come from inventory `previousSlugs`, `retired[].successor` and the adapter `redirectSources[]`); never a blanket redirect to the homepage; retired paths 410.
- **URL-009** · P0 · html, site — On each indexable page: the same canonical string in the canonical tag, `og:url`, the page's own hreflang `href` and JSON-LD `WebPage.url`; every hreflang `href` equals the canonical of its target page (`value-mismatch`: field, expected, actual). Sitemap `<loc>` is judged only by IDX-001.
- **URL-011** · P0 · edge — Alias hosts (with `edge.production: true`): every host in `aliasHosts` plus the adapter's `defaultAliasHosts` (platform default domains such as `<project>.vercel.app`, `<site>.netlify.app`, `<project>.pages.dev`; `*.` entries are resolved to concrete hosts from the platform config, unresolved ones are `notConfigured(aliasHosts)`) answers `/` and 3 indexable paths (drawn from the HTTP sample, 04 § 10, by the PRNG seeded with the lock digest) with **either** one 301/308 hop to the same path and query on the canonical origin, **or** 200 with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`. A 200 without noindex on an alias host is a duplicate site.
- **URL-012** · P0 · html — Syndicated content: a page matching a `syndicated` entry carries **either** `noindex` **or** a canonical equal to the expanded `originalUrl` (not both, MET-006), and `<main>` contains a visible `<a href>` to the expanded `originalUrl`. Such pages are not indexable pages (§ 2); KW-* ignore them. Cross-host canonicals on any other page fail URL-004.
- **URL-013** · P1 · http, html — Docs versions (with `docs.versions` set): for every 200 page whose path matches an `older` pattern, its equivalent latest path is `latest` joined by `/` with the remainder of the path after the pattern's literal prefix (WCF-008; `/docs/v1/a/b` → `/docs/a/b`). The page is **either** `noindex` **or**, when the equivalent latest path returns 200, canonical to that path's canonical (not both, MET-006); with no 200 equivalent it must be `noindex`. Older-version pages in a sitemap are IDX-001 findings only (they are not indexable pages, § 2).
- **URL-014** · P1 · http, edge — Path-variant probes (the variants URL-002 does not send). For each service, on `/` and the first 3 indexable pages of the HTTP sample (04 § 10, sample order), request: (a) default documents — for a path ending in `/` or whose last segment has no file extension, the path with each ruleset `default-documents.json` entry (`index.html`, `index.htm`, `index.php`, `default.aspx`, `default.asp`) appended after a `/`; (b) the path with its first `/` doubled; (c) when the path contains percent-encoded octets, the same path with lowercase hex digits; (d) `;jsessionid=sg` appended to the last path segment. Each variant answers **either** 404 or 410, **or** one hop with `redirectStatus` to the canonical URL (URL-001), **or** 200 with a `<link rel="canonical">` equal to the canonical of the unmodified URL. A 200 with a missing or different canonical, a redirect chain or a redirect elsewhere is a finding (`value-mismatch`: variant URL, expected canonical, actual; `redirect-chain`; `redirect-target`). On a service whose `host` is not `none`, variants (a) and (b) are decided by the host platform, so locally they are `skipped(host-emulation)` and checked only in the edge pass. Edge pass (with `edge.production: true`): the same probes against each `production.origins` origin for `/` only.

## 5. Metadata — `MET-*`

- **MET-001** · P0 · html — Exactly one non-empty `head > title` (an SVG `<title>` is not counted), whose **length** — Unicode code points of its NFC text with whitespace collapsed and trimmed (the length rule of MET-002 and MET-004 too) — is ≤ `titleLength.hardMax`; unique within locale across indexable pages (compared after NFKC, case folding and whitespace collapse); equals the oracle on programmatic pages.
- **MET-002** · P1 · html — A non-empty `<meta name="description">` in `<head>` (duplicates: RTE-008; with several, the first is judged), length (MET-001) ≤ `descriptionLength.hardMax`, unique within locale across indexable pages (as MET-001); oracle on programmatic pages.
- **MET-003** · P0 · html — Exactly one `<main>` with exactly one `<h1>` on public pages (part `main`; `missing-element`, `duplicate-element`); part `skip` — in document order across the whole `<body>`, each `<hN>` after the first heading has N ≤ the previous heading's level + 1 (going up any number of levels is allowed; `value-mismatch` with both headings).
- **MET-004** · W · html — Title or description length (MET-001) outside its `warnMin`–`warnMax` range. Copy changes happen in content sources/templates, never by padding or truncating in code.
- **MET-005** · P1 · html, http, edge — `og:title` = `<title>`, `og:description` = description, `og:url` = canonical, `og:type`, `og:site_name`, `twitter:card`; `og:image` from content data or `defaultOgImage` (content data = the page's own `og:image` meta; absent on a page while `defaultOgImage` is null → finding `missing-element`, owner builder, never `notConfigured`), absolute https on a service origin or on a `hosts` entry with purpose `assets`, 200 `image/*`, ≥ 1200×630, ≤ 5,000,000 bytes (part `image-weight`, W: > 300,000 bytes); `og:image:width` and `og:image:height` equal the image's pixel size and `og:image:alt` is non-empty; `twitter:card` = `summary_large_image` when the image is ≥ 1200 px wide; `og:locale` only from `ogLocales`. Part `fetchers`: the first 10 indexable pages of the HTTP sample in URL order (04 § 10; in the edge pass the homepage, 04 § 2) requested with each social-preview user agent (ruleset `social-agents.json`: facebookexternalhit, Twitterbot, LinkedInBot, WhatsApp, Slackbot-LinkExpanding, Discordbot, TelegramBot) return 200 with the same `og:title`, `og:image` and `og:url` as the Chrome UA response; in the edge pass a 403/429/challenge for these agents is W (edges verify bots by IP).
- **MET-006** · P0 · html, http — Robots directives. Directives are the comma-separated, trimmed, lowercased tokens of every `<meta name>` `robots`, `googlebot` or `bingbot` `content` and of every `X-Robots-Tag` header value (a leading `<agent>:` scopes a header value to that agent; none means all agents); the directives that apply to an agent are the unscoped ones plus those of its meta name and its scope. (1) An indexable page (§ 2) has none of `noindex`, `none`, `nofollow` in any source (`forbidden-value`: source, token). (2) A public 200 page whose `indexRules` entry has `index: false` has `noindex` in at least one source and no `nofollow` or `none` (RTE-004 row `noindex,follow`; `forbidden-value` or `missing-element`). (3) No conflict: the directives that apply to one agent never contain `noindex` together with `index` or `all`, or `nofollow` together with `follow` (`value-mismatch`: both values and sources). (4) `noindex` or `none` on a page whose canonical differs from its own URL (04 § 10 normalisation) is `forbidden-value`. The other Robots cells of the RTE-004 table are owned by IDX-006 (404/410), IDX-007 (`app`, `api`), IDX-012 (site search), IDX-005 (non-production), URL-012 and URL-013 (syndicated, older docs).
- **MET-007** · P0 · html — `<html lang>` equals the page locale tag (04 § 15 *Page locale*) after ASCII case folding, exactly (`en` does not equal `en-US`); a URL with no URL locale under `localePrefix: "always"` fails; `<meta charset="utf-8">` (case-insensitive value) starts within the first 1024 bytes of the decoded body (`value-mismatch`, `missing-element`). Chinese-locale pages: SE-502 adds only its tag restriction.
- **MET-008** · P0 · html — Leak scan exactly as in 04 § 12 (template markers anywhere outside code elements; words like `undefined`/`TODO` only when they make up a whole title, meta, heading, attribute, fact or cell value) (part `main`); part `banned` — each `bannedPhrases` entry, matched whole-word, case-insensitive, NFKC, in the MET-011 scanned fields minus link `href`s, `placeholderAllow` entries exempt; part `i18n-keys` — a text node or attribute value equal to a flattened key (I18-009) of a catalogue matched by the adapter's `i18nCatalogs` globs (03 § 6); without catalogues `skipped(no-catalogues)`. Elements are excluded from all parts only when matched by a `leakIgnore` selector (signed policy; each excluded element is reported as W); the markup attribute `data-shipgate-ignore` has no effect. Failure class `leak` (field, value, URL). Lorem ipsum and other placeholder text, whole-value or not, is owned by MET-011.
- **MET-009** · P0 · html — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">` (no `user-scalable=no`, no `maximum-scale` < 2).
- **MET-010** · P1 · html — Part `favicon`: ≥ 1 `<link rel="icon">` returns 200 and is SVG, or decodes (ICO: its largest frame) to a square image whose side is a multiple of 48 px (measured on the decoded image, not the `sizes` attribute); part `apple-touch-icon` (W): `<link rel="apple-touch-icon">` present and 200. Failure classes `missing-element`, `status-mismatch`, `value-mismatch` (pixel size).
- **MET-011** · P0 · html, http, site, browser — Placeholder and starter residue (owner; MET-008 owns template markers). Scanned: visible text, `<title>`, meta description, `og:*`/`twitter:*` content, `alt`/`title`/`aria-label` values, JSON-LD string values and link `href`s of public pages, excluding the code elements of the 04 § 12 leak scan; matching is whole-word, case-insensitive, NFKC, against the pinned ruleset dictionary `placeholders.json` for the page's language (plus language-neutral entries; shape in § 2). Parts `lorem`, `starter`, `contact`, `image-host`, `social`, `scaffold-route`, `dead-anchor`, `copyright`:
  - `lorem` — lorem-ipsum sequences (`lorem ipsum`, `dolor sit amet`, `consectetur adipiscing`, and the per-locale filler lists of `placeholders.json`);
  - `starter` — framework starter strings (`Create Next App`, `Welcome to Astro`, `Vite + React`, `Get started by editing`, `Welcome to Nuxt`, `Edit src/App`, …) and favicons/`apple-touch-icon`s whose SHA-256 is a framework default listed in `placeholders.json` (http fetch, as MET-010);
  - `contact` — fictional phone numbers (the fictional ranges of `placeholders.json`, e.g. NANP `555-0100`–`555-0199`, UK Ofcom drama ranges), placeholder addresses (`123 Main St`, `1234 Street Name`), e-mail addresses or links on reserved domains (`example.com`/`.org`/`.net`, any `*.example`, `*.test`, `*.invalid`, `*.localhost`; RFC 2606/6761), names `Your Company`, `Company Name`, `Your Name`, `John Doe`, `Jane Doe`, and `Acme` unless `brand` or `siteName` contains it;
  - `image-host` — `src`/`srcset`/`og:image` on placeholder image hosts (`placeholders.json`: `placehold.co`, `via.placeholder.com`, `placeholder.com`, `dummyimage.com`, `picsum.photos`, `source.unsplash.com`, `loremflickr.com`, `placekitten.com`, …);
  - `social` — an `<a href>` to a host in ruleset `social-hosts.json` (`[{ host, aliases[], handleSegment, sharePaths[] }]`: `aliases` are further hosts of the same network, `handleSegment` the 0-based index of the path segment holding the handle, `sharePaths` path prefixes of share-intent links, which are excluded) whose path is empty or `/`, or whose handle (the path segment at `handleSegment`) is in ruleset `placeholder-handles.json` (`[string]`, compared case-insensitively); when `business.json` `socialProfiles` is non-empty, every such link must equal one of its entries after normalisation (https, lowercase host, `www.`/`m.` dropped, query, fragment and trailing slash removed);
  - `scaffold-route` (P1, site) — an indexable 200 URL with a path segment (percent-decoded, case-insensitive, whole segment) in ruleset `scaffold-routes.json` (`test`, `demo`, `playground`, `styleguide`, `components`, `kitchen-sink`, `example`, `sandbox`, …), unless the path or the segment is in `placeholderAllow`;
  - `dead-anchor` (P1, browser) — a visible `<a>` whose `href` is absent, empty, `#` or `javascript:` on a browser-sampled page is a dead control (04 § 15, clicked in the control click probe). This part owns `<a>` elements on public pages; every other control, and `<a>` on `app` pages, is UX-006 (ux pack);
  - `copyright` (P1) — the holder in a `©`/`(c)`/`Copyright` notice (04 § 15 copyright holder) contains none of `brand`, `siteName`, `business.json` `name`/`legalName`, or a `placeholderAllow` entry.

  Strings listed in `placeholderAllow` are exempt.
- **MET-012** · P1 · html, browser — Real image sources: every content `<img>` in raw `<main>` (excluding `alt=""`, `role="presentation"`/`"none"`, `aria-hidden="true"`) has a `src` or `srcset` (or a `<picture><source srcset>`) that is not a `data:` URI and does not resolve to an image ≤ 2×2 px (lazy-load placeholders with the real URL only in `data-src` fail). In the browser, each such image after `element.scrollIntoView()` (no wheel or touch events) and network idle (≤ 3 s) has `complete` true, `naturalWidth > 2` and a non-`data:` `currentSrc`. Broken (4xx/5xx) images are CQ-003.
- **MET-013** · W · html — Title quality: part `generic` — after removing the brand suffix, the title is not empty, not only `brand`/`siteName`, and not an entry of ruleset `generic-titles.json` for the page locale (`Home`, `Untitled`, `Page`, `New Page`, `Document`, `Index`; compared after NFKC and case folding); part `suffix` — across indexable non-home pages, titles ending in a separator (` | `, ` - `, ` – `, ` — `, ` · `, ` : `) followed by `brand` or `siteName` use the most common separator + suffix pair (ties → the pair that sorts first by the UTF-8 bytes of separator followed by suffix; pages without a suffix are not findings); part `h1` — the title and the `<h1>` share ≥ 1 term after lowercasing, NFKC, removing the brand suffix and the page locale's stop words (ruleset `stop-words.json`), and Snowball stemming (as KW-002).

## 6. Structured data — `SCH-*`

**Allowed types by surface:** public pages: `WebSite`, `WebPage` (+ `AboutPage`, `ContactPage`, `CollectionPage`, `FAQPage`*), `Organization` (+ `LocalBusiness` subtypes), `BreadcrumbList`, `Article`/`BlogPosting`/`NewsArticle`, `Product`, `ProductGroup`, `Offer`, `OfferShippingDetails`, `MerchantReturnPolicy`, `Service`, `ContactPoint`, `OpeningHoursSpecification`, `SoftwareApplication`, `Event`, `Person`, `Place`, `PostalAddress`, `GeoCoordinates`, `ItemList`, `VideoObject`, `ImageObject`, `Review`/`AggregateRating`*; plus the `schemaTypes` of every `search-engines.features` entry whose row in `rules/features/google.json` has `status: active` (e.g. `JobPosting`, `Recipe`, `Course`, `Dataset`, `DiscussionForumPosting`, `ProfilePage`), and the value types reachable as property values of allowed nodes (`ListItem`, `Question`, `Answer`, `Brand`, `Rating`, `QuantitativeValue`, `MonetaryAmount`, `DefinedRegion`, `ShippingDeliveryTime`; a node of a value type at the top of `@graph` is not allowed). A node of a deprecated feature's type is judged only by SE-101 `deprecated`. Programmatic pages: the family's `schemaTypes`, never `Review`, `AggregateRating`, `FAQPage`, `HowTo`, `ClaimReview`, `SpecialAnnouncement`.
\* `FAQPage` only when the Q&A is visible on the page (Google stopped showing FAQ rich results in May 2026; the markup is harmless but earns no rich result, so SE-101 reports it as W). `Review`/`AggregateRating` only on a page matching a `reviewSources[].pattern`, and never on (or as `review`/`aggregateRating` of) an `Organization`/`LocalBusiness` node (self-serving). An `AggregateRating`'s `ratingValue` equals the `value`, its `reviewCount` (or `ratingCount`) the `count`, and its `bestRating` (absent = 5) the `best` (absent = 5) of the `business.json` `ratings[]` entry named by that `reviewSources` entry's `ratingsSource`, compared as decimal numbers. A `Review` node has no `reviewRating` and passes UX-014 part `testimonials` (its `reviewBody` and `author.name` match a `business.json` `testimonials[]` entry). A node that fails these conditions, or any such node while `reviewSources` is empty, is `forbidden-value` (node, property) under SCH-003, never `notConfigured`.

- **SCH-001** · P0 · html — One `<script type="application/ld+json">` with `@graph`; stable absolute `@id`s (`{origin}/#organization`, `{origin}/#website`, `{canonical}#webpage`, `{canonical}#breadcrumb`, `{canonical}#primary`, `{canonical}#localbusiness` on location pages, LOC-003); every `@id` reference resolves. Required nodes: every indexable public page has exactly one such block, containing a `WebPage` node (or a `WebPage` subtype allowed above) `{canonical}#webpage` and a `WebSite` node or `@id` reference `{origin}/#website`; `/` carries the full `Organization` node `{origin}/#organization` (other pages may reference it by `@id`); a `BreadcrumbList` `{canonical}#breadcrumb` exactly when a visible breadcrumb exists (SCH-008). Missing node or block → `missing-element`; a second block → `duplicate-element`; an unresolved reference → `value-mismatch`.
- **SCH-002** · P0† · ast, html — Serialised only by `safeJsonLd()` (escapes `<`, `>`, `&`, U+2028, U+2029; adapter codegen provides it per language); raw script text has no case-insensitive `</script`; parses.
- **SCH-003** · P0 · html — Only the allowed types above for the surface/family, and `Review`/`AggregateRating` only as the footnote above allows (`forbidden-value`: node `@type`, URL).
- **SCH-004** · P0 · html — Required properties:

  | Type | Required |
  |---|---|
  | Organization | `@id`, `name`, `url`, `logo` (≥ 112×112, answers 200 `image/*`, not disallowed for Googlebot by the 04 § 15 robots.txt evaluation) |
  | WebSite | `@id`, `url`, `name`, `publisher` → `#organization` |
  | WebPage | `@id`, `url` (= canonical), `name` (= title), `inLanguage`, `isPartOf` → `#website`; `breadcrumb` when a breadcrumb exists |
  | BreadcrumbList | items with `position` 1..n, `name`, `item` (absolute; last may omit) |
  | Article / BlogPosting | `headline` (= h1), `datePublished`, `author` (Person/Organization with `name` and `url`), `image`; `dateModified` when shown |
  | Product | `name`, `image`, and `offers` (with `price`, `priceCurrency`, `availability`) or a `review`/`aggregateRating` allowed by the § 6 footnote |
  | Offer | `price`, `priceCurrency`, `availability`, `url` |
  | Service | `@id`, `name`, `provider` → `#organization`, `areaServed` |
  | LocalBusiness | `@id`, `name`, `url`, `address` (`streetAddress`, `addressLocality`, `postalCode`, `addressCountry`), `telephone` when shown |
  | SoftwareApplication | `name`, `applicationCategory`, `operatingSystem`, `offers` |
  | Event | `name`, `startDate`, `location`, `eventStatus`, `eventAttendanceMode` |
  | VideoObject | `name`, `description`, `thumbnailUrl`, `uploadDate`, `contentUrl` or `embedUrl` |
  | ItemList | `itemListElement` with `position` and `url` |

- **SCH-005** · P0 · html — No `""`, `null`, `[]`, `{}` values; no `0,0` coordinates; no invented dates: every `datePublished`, `dateModified`, `uploadDate` and `startDate` value either parses back (04 § 11) from a visible date on the page (04 § 15 visible dates), or equals a `date`/`datetime` field of the page's inventory row, or equals the page's content modification time (IDX-003); otherwise `forbidden-value` (property, value).
- **SCH-006** · P0 · html, browser — These properties of `Product`, `Offer`, `LocalBusiness`, `Event`, `Review`, `AggregateRating`, `Person` and `Service` nodes and of the `{canonical}#primary` node appear in visible text (§ 2; on browser-sampled pages also in the browser's visible text): `name`, `address` subproperties, `price`/`lowPrice`/`highPrice`, `startDate`, `datePublished`, `dateModified`, `ratingValue`, `reviewCount`, `telephone`, `openingHoursSpecification` (`WebPage`, `WebSite` and `BreadcrumbList` are judged by MET-001, SCH-004 and SCH-008, not here). Comparison: names and addresses by normalised containment (NFKC, case-folded, whitespace-collapsed); prices, dates and ratings by parse-back of visible text (04 § 11) compared as values; `telephone` by E.164 normalisation of visible phone text and `tel:` links (default region: the address country, else the `defaultLocale` region); `openingHoursSpecification` (`dayOfWeek`, `opens`, `closes`) by parse-back of visible hours (CLDR day names and time patterns of the page locale, 24-hour comparison). Omitted page sections are omitted from JSON-LD.
- **SCH-007** · P0 · html, data — `LocalBusiness` only with a verified physical location (programmatic: `localBusiness` fields; elsewhere a `locations[]` entry of `quality/data/business.json` whose `pagePath` is the page, LOC-003); service-area pages use `Service` + `areaServed`.
- **SCH-008** · P1 · html — `BreadcrumbList` mirrors the visible breadcrumb (`<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb"><ol>`) item for item: same count; each `name` equals the link text (NFKC, whitespace-collapsed); each `item` equals the canonicalised `href` (the last item may have no link). Failure classes `missing-element`, `value-mismatch`.
- **SCH-009** · P1 · html — Pages whose `<main>`, before its first `<h2>`, contains a `<video>` (its `src` or first `<source src>`) or an `<iframe>` whose `src` host is a `hosts` entry with purpose `video` carry a `VideoObject` whose `contentUrl` or `embedUrl` equals that `src` (absolute, canonicalised); its `contentUrl` and `thumbnailUrl` are not disallowed for Googlebot (04 § 15 robots.txt evaluation). Failure classes `missing-element`, `value-mismatch`, `forbidden-value` (robots-blocked media URL).
- **SCH-010** · W · html, browser — Article pages (indexable pages with `Article`/`BlogPosting`/`NewsArticle` JSON-LD) show a visible author name equal (NFKC, case-folded, normalised containment) to the JSON-LD `author.name`. Owner of the visible-author check (CQ-004 is a tombstone); visible dates are SE-110. Failure classes `missing-element`, `value-mismatch`.
- **SCH-011** · P1 (part `domain` W) · html — Vocabulary and value formats, against the schema.org version pinned in ruleset `schemaorg.json` (§ 2 shape). Part `vocabulary`: every `@type` and property exists in it (`forbidden-value`: type or property). Part `domain` (W): a property used on a type outside its `domainIncludes`, supertypes included (`value-mismatch`). Part `format`: values of `Date`/`DateTime`-ranged properties are ISO 8601 (DateTime with offset); `Duration`-ranged properties are ISO 8601 durations (`PT1H30M`); `price`/`lowPrice`/`highPrice` are a JSON number or a string matching `^\d+(\.\d+)?$` (no symbols, no grouping); `priceCurrency`/`currency` are ISO 4217 codes; `availability`, `itemCondition`, `eventStatus`, `eventAttendanceMode`, `dayOfWeek` are schema.org enumeration member URLs (`https://schema.org/InStock`; `http://` accepted; `dayOfWeek` also accepts the bare member name, `Monday`); `url`, `@id`, `image`, `logo`, `sameAs`, `item`, `contentUrl`, `embedUrl`, `thumbnailUrl` are absolute http(s) URLs (`value-mismatch`: property, value).

## 7. Internal links — `LNK-*`

- **LNK-001** · P0 · browser — Navigation to indexable pages uses plain `<a href>`: in the rendered DOM of browser-sampled pages (04 § 10), no element without a usable `href` (`href` absent, empty, `#` or `javascript:`; an `<a>` so marked, an element with a registered `click` listener, `role="link"`, or a `<button>` outside a `<form>`) navigates to an indexable page when clicked in the control click probe (04 § 15 dead control). Such an `<a>` that does nothing is MET-011 `dead-anchor`; one that navigates to an indexable page is judged here. Failure class `pattern-match` (selector, target URL). Raw-HTML presence of links is RTE-001 `links`.
- **LNK-002** · P0 · site — Every internal link target is a canonical indexable 200 (or a declared non-index path); no links to redirects or 404s; no `rel="nofollow"` on internal links.
- **LNK-003** · P0 · site — No orphans: every indexable page has ≥ 1 inbound link from another indexable page and is reachable from `/` within `maxClickDepth` clicks. Inbound links and click depth are computed over the raw-HTML `a[href]` graph of the crawl (04 § 10; targets canonicalised); links that exist only in the rendered DOM do not count (RTE-001 `links` reports them). Failure classes `orphan` (URL), `budget-exceeded` (URL, depth).
- **LNK-004** · P1 · html — Anchor text names the destination, for internal and outbound `a[href]` on indexable pages. Anchor text = the link's accessible name (text, else `aria-label`, else the `alt` of a contained image), NFKC, case-folded, whitespace-collapsed; it is not empty, not a bare URL, not an entry of the page locale's generic anchor-text list (ruleset `generic-anchors.json`: "click here", "read more", "here", "link", …), and not a list of > 5 comma-separated terms. Failure class `forbidden-value` (anchor text, URL).
- **LNK-005** · W · site — Each page listed in `targets` is linked from ≥ 3 distinct indexable pages other than itself, each with an `a[href]` whose anchor text (LNK-004 definition) contains the target query's head term (§ 12); several links on one page count once (`missing-element`: target URL, number of linking pages).
- **LNK-006** · W · site — Outbound links (opt-in `externalLinks.check`; `profile: nightly`): the distinct absolute `<a href>` URLs on indexable pages whose host is no service origin, up to `externalLinks.maxUrls` (ordered by number of linking pages, then URL), are requested with HEAD (GET when HEAD answers 405/501), following ≤ 5 redirects, ≤ 1 request/s per host, 10 s timeout. Final 404/410 → W listing the linking pages. 403, 429, 5xx, timeouts and TLS errors → `unknown`, not reported. Runs from the verify machine under the TR-106 network exception.
- **LNK-007** · P1 · site, diff — Links added in the diff resolve (guard against invented citations; with `externalLinks.checkAdded`, default profile, `scope: changed`): the candidates are absolute http(s) `<a href>` URLs on crawled pages whose host is no service origin and whose host + path occurs in a line added by the diff (04 § 5 diff base) and in no line removed by it. ≤ 50 requests per run (candidates ordered by URL; the rest are listed as unchecked in the coverage record), ≤ 1 request/s per host, HEAD (GET when HEAD answers 405/501), ≤ 5 redirects, 10 s timeout. A finding when the host or a redirect hop's host answers NXDOMAIN, or the final status is 404/410. 403, 429, 5xx, timeouts, TLS errors and other DNS failures (SERVFAIL, no network) → `unknown`, not reported. Network access under TR-106 (links added in the diff). No git or no diff base → `skipped(no-git)`.

## 8. Indexation and crawler policy — `IDX-*`

- **IDX-001** · P0† · site — Sitemap URL set equals the set of indexable pages (§ 2) in canonical form (URL-001); each discrepancy class (extra, missing, non-canonical, non-200, noindex) is its own finding. Owner of every sitemap-membership check (syndicated, older-docs, search-result and `<loc>`-is-canonical cases included).
- **IDX-002** · P0 · site — ≤ `maxUrlsPerFile` (hard limit 50,000 URLs / 50 MB) per file; sitemap index when > 1 file; valid escaped XML; absolute URLs on the right origin; unique entries.
- **IDX-003** · P1 · site — `<lastmod>` (W3C datetime with time and timezone, SE-004) is the page's **content modification time**, or omitted. Content modification time: for family pages the inventory `lastmod` (family `lastmodField`); for other pages the latest committer time among the route's file (adapter `routes.fileMap`) and the files of its static import graph (04 § 13) that have no role `layout`, `style`, `config`, `asset` or `test` and are imported by ≤ 50 % of route files (working-tree changes count as the reference time, 04 § 15); a page whose graph contains an adapter `queryApis`/`httpClientApis` call (content from a database or CMS) has none. When the page's `mainTextHash` (03 § 12) equals its entry in `_main/routes.jsonl`, the content modification time is that entry's `lastmod` instead, when it has one (a code-only change does not move it; AEO-012). No git → non-family pages have no content modification time: part `future` is `skipped(no-git)` for them, part `build-time` runs on the `<lastmod>` instants alone, and part `oracle` is unaffected. Part `build-time`: > 50 % of a sitemap's `<lastmod>` values lie within 10 minutes of the G3 build start, or a sitemap with ≥ 10 entries has one identical `<lastmod>` instant on all of them; part `oracle`: on family pages `<lastmod>` equals the inventory `lastmod` as an instant to the second; part `future`: on other pages with a content modification time, `<lastmod>` is not later than it + 24 h; part `deprecated-tags`: no `changefreq`/`priority` (ignored by Google and Bing). Failure classes `value-mismatch` (URL, expected, actual), `forbidden-value`.
- **IDX-004** · P0 · http — Production `robots.txt`: 200, `text/plain`; references the sitemap index; never disallows sitemap URLs or URLs relying on `noindex`; never blocks CSS/JS/framework assets or images used on indexable pages. All allow/disallow decisions are the 04 § 15 robots.txt evaluation for the `Googlebot` and `Bingbot` tokens (`search-agents.json`); a URL blocked for either is a finding (`forbidden-value`: rule line, URL, token).
- **IDX-005** · P0† · http, edge — Environment mode from `DEPLOY_ENV` only. Non-production (checked in the preview pass, 04 § 2 item 4, on the `preview-20` sample and `/robots.txt`): `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, `Disallow: /`, no sitemap. Production (checked in the main G4 pass, `DEPLOY_ENV=production`, on the HTTP sample, `/robots.txt` and the sitemaps): none of these; no URL whose host is the `previewUrl` host, `localhost`, `*.localhost`, an address in `127.0.0.0/8`, `::1`, an RFC 1918 address or a host of an adapter `defaultAliasHosts` entry (resolved as URL-011), in any HTML attribute, JSON-LD value, sitemap, robots.txt or `Location` header (`leak`). Part `preview-edge`: in the edge pass, `previewUrl` is either protected (a request without the `edge.bypassHeaderVar` header answers 401/403 or redirects to a host other than the preview host) or, on `/robots.txt` and the homepage, sends `X-Robots-Tag` containing `noindex` and serves a robots.txt whose `*` group has `Disallow: /`.
- **IDX-006** · P0 · html — 404/410 responses carry `noindex` and no canonical or hreflang.
- **IDX-007** · P0 · http — `app` and `api` surfaces are never in the sitemap and send `noindex` (meta or `X-Robots-Tag`).
- **IDX-008** · P1 · cfg, http — AI agents. The pinned ruleset `ai-agents.json` (§ 2 shape: `token`, `vendor`, `category` `training`|`search`|`user-fetch`, `userAgent`, `honoursRobots`, `docUrl`) lists at least GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Google-Extended, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot, meta-externalagent, meta-webindexer, DuckAssistBot, MistralAI-User, Google-Agent (`user-fetch`). `honoursRobots` is set from the vendor's documentation cited in `docUrl` at ruleset build time (`false` for Google-Agent: Google documents that user-triggered fetchers generally ignore robots.txt). Parts `match`, `visibility`, `search-engines`, `split`, `unenforceable`:
  - `match` — for every token with `honoursRobots: true`, the effective robots.txt decision for each indexable URL (04 § 15 robots.txt evaluation) equals `aiCrawlers[token]` (unlisted tokens: `allow`); tokens with `honoursRobots: false` are `notApplicable` here (an equal decision proves nothing);
  - `visibility` (cfg) — with `aiVisibility: true`, no `search` or `user-fetch` token is `disallow` in `aiCrawlers` (training tokens are the Operator's choice);
  - `search-engines` — Googlebot and Bingbot are never disallowed from indexable pages by an AI-agent group;
  - `split` (W) — a vendor's `search` token is disallowed while its `training` token is allowed;
  - `unenforceable` (W, cfg) — a token with `honoursRobots: false` set to `disallow` in `aiCrawlers`: robots.txt does not restrict it, so the restriction needs an edge rule (`answer-engines.botProtection.provider`, AEO-008 review item) (`forbidden-value`: token).

  Failure classes: `value-mismatch` (`match`: token, URL, expected, actual), `forbidden-value` (the other parts).

  Google AI Overviews and AI Mode use Googlebot: `Google-Extended` controls only Gemini training/grounding, never Search visibility.
- **IDX-009** · P1 · site — With `sitemap.images`/`videos` enabled (else `notApplicable`): each `<image:loc>`/`<video:content_loc>` is listed under a `<url>` that is an indexable page, answers 200 with `image/*`/`video/*` (`<video:player_loc>`: 200 `text/html`), is not disallowed for Googlebot (04 § 15 robots.txt evaluation) and occurs as a `src`, `srcset`, `<source>` or `og:image` value in that page's raw HTML (`forbidden-value`: entry, violated condition; `value-mismatch`).
- **IDX-010** · W · http — `llms.txt` per `llmsTxt` (default `required` when `project.kinds` includes `docs`, else `optional`): when `required`, `/llms.txt` returns 200 `text/plain`/markdown and links only to indexable canonical URLs; `optional`: when `/llms.txt` returns 200, the same link check applies (a missing file passes); `off`: not checked.
- **IDX-011** · P0 · edge — Production launch check (with `project.stage: "live"` and `edge.production: true`, otherwise `notApplicable`; bounded read-only GET under 04 § 2 item 5): for each public service origin listed in `production.origins`, fetch `/robots.txt`, the sitemaps it lists (else `/sitemap.xml`; for an index, ≤ 10 child files in document order), `/`, and 20 `<loc>` URLs sampled from the fetched sitemaps (PRNG seeded with the lock digest). Fails when: robots.txt is not 200 or disallows `/` for Googlebot or Bingbot (effective group, RFC 9309); a fetched sitemap is not 200 XML; `/` or a sampled URL is not 200 without redirect; any of them carries `noindex`/`none` in meta robots (`robots`, `googlebot`, `bingbot`) or `X-Robots-Tag`; or its canonical is not on the same production origin. ≤ 33 requests per origin, 1 request/s.
- **IDX-012** · P1 · http, html, ast — Internal site search: result URLs are not indexable. Search routes are routes whose handler reads a free-text query parameter (`q`, `s`, `query`, `search`, `keyword`, or adapter `searchHandlers`), plus the same-origin `action` of `GET` forms that contain `<input type="search">` or sit in `role="search"`. Probed with `?<param>=shipgate-probe`: the response is `noindex` (meta or `X-Robots-Tag`) or its path is robots-disallowed; no search result URL appears in a canonical tag of another page (sitemap membership: IDX-001; the result page itself carries no canonical: URL-004).
- **IDX-013** · P1 · http — Machine-text variants: `.md`/`.txt` siblings of HTML pages (`{path}.md`, `{path}/index.md`), URLs advertised by `<link rel="alternate" type="text/markdown">`, responses negotiated by `Accept: text/markdown` and `/llms-full.txt`, when they return 200: each per-page variant sends `Link: <html canonical>; rel="canonical"` or `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, and `/llms-full.txt` (many pages combined, no single canonical) sends `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`; per-page variants' text overlaps the page's raw `<main>` text ≥ `parity.minOverlap` (04 § 12 token overlap); a negotiated response on an HTML URL sends `Vary: Accept`.
- **IDX-014** · W · http — When any `training` token is `disallow` in `aiCrawlers`, robots.txt also states it as a Content Signal (ruleset `content-signals.json` syntax, e.g. `Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-train=no` in the `*` group); with `aiVisibility: true` no signal says `search=no` or `ai-input=no`.
- **IDX-015** · P1 · http, html — Feeds (when indexable pages carry `Article`/`BlogPosting`/`NewsArticle` JSON-LD): the article index (the indexable 200 page at the longest common path prefix of those pages, else `/`) and every article page carry ≥ 1 `<link rel="alternate">` with `type` `application/rss+xml` or `application/atom+xml`. Each advertised feed returns 200 with an XML content type and parses as RSS 2.0 (`<rss version="2.0">` with channel `title`, `link`, `description`; each `item` with `link`) or Atom 1.0 (`feed` with `id`, `title`, `updated`; each `entry` with `id`, `title`, `updated`, `link rel="alternate"`). Every item link equals the canonical of an indexable 200 page; when that page has `datePublished`, the item date (RSS `pubDate`, Atom `published`, else `updated`) equals it as an instant to the second (a date-only `datePublished` compares by day in `timeZone`).
- **IDX-016** · P1 · browser — Confirmation pages (both checks run in G6 on the flow's output): for each form submission that a flow proves with a UX-001 outcome step, the document URL when that outcome step completes, if its path differs from the URL before the submitting step (thank-you or confirmation page), is served with `noindex` (meta robots or `X-Robots-Tag`), and that URL, in canonical form, is absent from every sitemap fetched in G4.

## 9. Localisation — `I18-*`

- **I18-001** · P0 · html — One locale → no `hreflang`.
- **I18-002** · P0 · site — Multiple locales: reciprocal clusters including self; only versions of the same entity: same family + `rowId` (inventory `alternates`) for family pages; for other pages the same route pattern with the same param values after removing the locale segment (§ 1 *Locale segment*), or an entry of the i18n path map the adapter `config` readers return (translated slugs, e.g. Nuxt i18n `pages`); `x-default` only when set and resolving; valid BCP-47 (`en-GB`, not `en-UK`); targets canonical indexable 200; same map in HTML and sitemap if both are used.
- **I18-003** · P0 · html — Each locale page is self-canonical.
- **I18-004** · W · review — No machine-translated or synthesized locale content in place of source content (not mechanically detectable; for programmatic families, DAT-* already forbid content not present in the locale's dataset).
- **I18-005** · P1 · html — Crawlable links between locale versions; cookies/`Accept-Language` never the only route; a request for each locale's page with the Googlebot UA (`search-agents.json`) and `Accept-Language` set to each other declared locale returns the same status and `Location` as without the header (`status-mismatch`; IP-based redirects are not checked: the verify machine has one IP).
- **I18-006** · P1 · ast — Explicit locale formatting: in files of role route, layout or component (04 § 7.1), each call of an adapter `formatterApis` entry whose ApiRef `args` declares a `locale` role passes that argument, and it is neither omitted nor the literal `undefined`/`null`; entries that declare a `timeZone` role also pass `timeZone`; the `currency` argument of money formatters (entries declaring a `currency` role) is not a string literal (currency comes from data) (`pattern-match`: call, missing or literal role). A `formatterApis` entry without a `locale` role → `noAdapterSupport` for its calls.
- **I18-007** · P1 · html — The language of `<main>` text (pages with ≥ 50 words, UAX #29), detected with the pinned `lingua` crate (WP-102 toolchain, version in the report; high-accuracy mode, minimum relative distance 0.25), equals the primary subtag of `<html lang>`; no confident result → not checked for that page (listed in the coverage record). Failure class `value-mismatch` (detected vs declared).
- **I18-008** · P1 · html — Pages of a locale whose CLDR `characterOrder` (toolchain CLDR) is `right-to-left` set `dir="rtl"` on `<html>` (`missing-element`).
- **I18-009** · P1 · data, html — Complete translations (multiple locales). Part `catalogue` (data): with message catalogues (adapter `i18nCatalogs`, locale from each glob's `{locale}` capture, 03 § 6; none → `skipped(no-catalogues)`), every leaf key of the `defaultLocale` catalogue (nested keys flattened with `.`; plural variants compared by base key) exists with a non-empty string value in every other locale's catalogue. Part `untranslated` (html): on sampled pages of a non-default locale, the text (NFKC, whitespace-collapsed, case-folded) of each link in `<nav>` and `<footer>` and of each `button`, `[role=button]` and `input[type=submit|button]` value must not equal a default-locale string: a value of the `defaultLocale` catalogue, or, without catalogues, the text of an element of the same kind on the page's `defaultLocale` alternate (I18-002 cluster; no alternate → not checked). Exempt: `brand`, `siteName`, words in the `content.dictionary` file, strings ≤ 2 characters, and strings that are the value of the same key in the page locale's catalogue.
- **I18-010** · P1 · site, html — French for Quebec (with `ca-qc` in `privacy.jurisdictions`, else `notApplicable`; Charter of the French Language as amended by Bill 96: French offered at least as favourably). The **French alternate** of a page is the member of its I18-002 cluster whose locale is `fr-CA`, else `fr`. Part `alternate`: every indexable public page whose locale is not French has a French alternate that answers 200, is an indexable page and is self-canonical (`missing-element`: page URL); a single-locale non-French site fails this part for every page. Part `complete`: the I18-009 checks run on the French alternates (I18-009 `catalogue` when catalogues exist; I18-009 `untranslated` always, against the `defaultLocale` alternate when there are no catalogues), and their findings on those pages are reported under this part. Part `parity`: each JSON-LD `Offer.price` (compared as a decimal) and each `tel:` (E.164-normalised) and `mailto:` (case-folded) target of a page also occurs on its French alternate (`value-mismatch`: value, both URLs). Part `prominence`: on each non-French page, the page header (the first `<header>` that is not inside `<main>`, `<article>`, `<aside>` or `<section>`; none → the `<body>` content before `<main>`) contains an `a[href]` to the French alternate that precedes, in document order, every `a[href]` to another member of the cluster (`missing-element`, `value-mismatch`).

## 10. Content quality (non-programmatic pages) — `CQ-*`

- **CQ-001** · W · html — Thin pages: indexable pages with < 150 words (UAX #29) of `<main>` text whose `<main>` contains no non-decorative `<img>` (decorative as MET-012), `<video>`, `<iframe>`, `<table>` with ≥ 2 rows, or `<ul>`/`<ol>` with ≥ 3 `<li>`.
- **CQ-002** · P1 · content — Exact duplicates: two indexable URLs, neither of them a family or hub page (pairs with one: CNT-005), with equal `mainTextHash` (03 § 12 `routes.jsonl` definition, computed for this run's crawl). Needs a complete crawl, else `skipped(incomplete-crawl)` (04 § 10). Failure class `similarity` (both URLs).
- **CQ-003** · P1 · html — Broken images on indexable pages (4xx/5xx `src`).
- **CQ-004** · W · html — Tombstone (deprecated): the visible author on article pages is owned by SCH-010, visible dates by SE-110.
- **CQ-005** · W · html — Images in `<main>` whose file-name stem (before the extension, percent-decoded) matches `^(IMG|DSC|DCIM|PXL|Screenshot)[ _-]?\d+`, `^image ?\(\d+\)$`, `^[0-9a-f]{16,}$` or a UUID, while the `alt` has ≥ 2 words (descriptive filenames help image search; `pattern-match`). Patterns are matched case-insensitively.
- **CQ-006** · P1 · baseline, site — Bulk page additions (scaled-content control for pages outside programmatic families): the new pages are the indexable canonical URLs of this run that are not family or hub pages and are absent from `_main/routes.jsonl` (`skipped(no-main)` without it). When their count exceeds `content.maxNewPagesPerChange`, every new page must be listed in an unexpired Operator-signed approval `quality/approvals/pages-<n>.json` (03 § 14), which records how the pages were made (`aiProvenance`: model, prompt version, reviewer, review date) and whether they carry an AI disclosure (`disclosure`); both are copied into the report. Unlisted pages are a finding whose owner is `operator`; when it is the only blocking finding, the handoff is `operator-owned` and its `decisionsNeeded` names `approve pages` (04 § 1.9). Agents never write approvals.
- **CQ-007** · W · html — Spelling: words (UAX #29, NFKC) in `<title>`, meta description, headings and `<main>` text of indexable pages are checked with the toolchain's Hunspell dictionary for the page locale, extended by the `content.dictionary` file (one word per line), `brand`, `siteName`, `business.json` names and the text values of programmatic dataset fields. Skipped: tokens with digits, all-caps tokens ≤ 5 letters, URLs, e-mail addresses, and text in the leak-scan code elements (04 § 12). W when a page has > 3 unknown words (listed). A page locale without a toolchain dictionary is never skipped: 04 § 15 *Page locale* (no entry) applies, with the Hunspell dictionary as the list.
- **CQ-008** · P1 · content — Hand-made doorways: pages outside programmatic families and hubs that differ only by swapped-in words (algorithm: 04 § 15, swap similarity). A connected component of ≥ 5 swap-similar indexable pages in one route group is a finding (`similarity`) that lists its pages and the substituted words. Needs a complete crawl, else `skipped(incomplete-crawl)` (04 § 10). Pages of a declared family are excluded. The fix is to declare the set as a programmatic family with data, or merge the pages. This rule owns swap-only near-duplicates outside families; CNT-012 owns plain near-duplicates.
- **CQ-009** · W · html — Stale years (reference year = the year of the reference time, 04 § 15). Part `title-year`: on an indexable page, `<title>` or `<h1>` contains a 4-digit token 1900–2099 below the reference year and a word of ruleset `evergreen-words.json` for the page locale (`best`, `top`, `guide`, `review`, …), unless the year of the page's `dateModified` (else `datePublished`) is ≤ that token. Part `copyright-year`: the end year of a `©`/`(c)`/`Copyright` notice (the single year or the last year of a range, 04 § 15 copyright holder) is below the reference year − 1.
- **CQ-010** · P1 · content — No copied third-party text: on each indexable page of the HTTP sample (04 § 10), the visible `<main>` text (UAX #29 words, NFKC, case-folded, punctuation-only tokens dropped) contains no run of ≥ 30 consecutive words that also occurs in a document of the **session fetch store** (03 § 15: the text of every web fetch, search result and browser-tool page the agent host reported in any hook-recorded session for this repo-id, 05 § 3) whose host is no service origin and no host of a `business.json` `socialProfiles` entry. Detection: 30-word shingles of both texts hashed (xxh3-64), candidate matches confirmed word by word. A run inside a `<blockquote>` or `<q>` with a `cite` attribute, or inside an element that also contains an `<a href>` to the fetched URL, is a quotation and passes. Missing input: no hook-recorded session for this repo-id (CI, hooks never installed), or only sessions of hosts whose 03 § 16 row reports no fetches (05 § 3.3) → `skipped(no-fetch-store)`; a recorded session of a host that reports fetch events with no store file → `missing-element`, owner builder (the state dir is a protected path, 05 § 3.1, so deleting the store is not an escape). Failure class `similarity` (page URL, fetched URL, the first 30 words of the run).

## 11. Trust pages — `TRS-*`

YMYL pages = pages matching `ymylPatterns`, plus every indexable page when `project.kinds` includes `ymyl`.

- **TRS-001** · P1 (part `strict` P0) · site, html — About and contact. The checks below run as part `strict` when `project.kinds` includes `local-business` or `ymyl` or `ymylPatterns` is non-empty, else as part `main`: `trustPages.about` and `trustPages.contact` return 200, are indexable, and are linked with plain `<a href>` from every indexable public page; the contact page shows (visible text, `tel:` or `mailto:` link) ≥ 1 telephone or e-mail equal to the homepage `Organization` `telephone`, `email` or `contactPoint[].telephone`/`.email` (phones E.164-normalised as SCH-006, e-mails case-folded), and to a `business.json` phone or e-mail when that file exists. With only one of the two sources, the contact page is compared with that one; when the homepage `Organization` has no `telephone`, `email` or `contactPoint` and `business.json` is absent, the match is a `missing-element` finding (owner builder), never `notConfigured`. Failure classes `status-mismatch`, `missing-element`, `value-mismatch`.
- **TRS-002** · P1 · html, browser — YMYL pages: a visible author name linked to an author page (200, indexable); JSON-LD `WebPage` carries `reviewedBy` (`Person`/`Organization` with `name`) and `lastReviewed` (ISO 8601 date), the `reviewedBy` name appears in visible text (normalised containment) and a visible date parses back to `lastReviewed` (SCH-006); `<main>` contains ≥ 1 `<a href>` to a host that is no service origin and not in the `hosts` registry with purpose `affiliate` or `advertising` (a cited source). Author markup is AEO-003.
- **TRS-003** · W · site, html — Editorial transparency (`project.kinds` includes `news` or `ymyl`, or `ymylPatterns` is non-empty): `trustPages.editorialPolicy` and `trustPages.corrections` are set, return 200, are indexable and are linked from every article and YMYL page; the homepage `Organization` has `publishingPrinciples` = the editorial-policy URL and `correctionsPolicy` = the corrections URL.
- **TRS-004** · W · review — Comparison pages: indexable pages whose path or `<title>` matches ruleset `comparison-patterns.json` for the page locale (`vs`, `versus`, `alternative(s)`, `compare`, `comparison`, …) are listed as review items; the Auditor confirms every claim about a competitor (price, feature, limit, rating) states an as-of date and cites a source.

## 12. Keyword targeting — `KW-*` (only when `targets` is set)

**Head term** of a `targets[].query` (KW-002, LNK-005): the query's words (UAX #29, NFKC, lowercased) minus the stop words of the target URL's locale (ruleset `stop-words.json`) and minus place names of the ruleset `gazetteer/<country>.json` of the locale's region (all gazetteers when the locale has no region; longest match, as 04 § 15 swap similarity); the remaining words Snowball-stemmed with the pinned stemmer of that locale (WP-104). A text contains the head term when its stemmed words (same normalisation) include all of them; a query whose head term is empty is checked against its full stemmed word list.

- **KW-001** · P1 · cfg, site — Each target query maps to exactly one URL (no two targets with the same query on different URLs: cannibalisation by design).
- **KW-002** · W · html — The target URL's `<title>` or `<h1>` contains the query's head term (`missing-element`, query and both texts).
