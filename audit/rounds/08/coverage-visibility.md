## Proposed rule text

**1 — search-engines · SE-010 (Must)**, insert after SE-009 in § 2.

- **SE-010** · P0 (part `unsupported` P1, part `crawl-delay` W) · http — Auxiliary crawler tokens and robots.txt directive hygiene. Google, Yandex and Baidu fetch images, favicons, video, news and shopping data with their own robots.txt tokens, and each token is decided by its own group, else by the `*` group (04 § 15 *robots.txt evaluation*; Google: "Even if there is an entry for a related crawler, it is only valid if it's specifically matching"), so SE-002's `Googlebot` decision says nothing about them. Ruleset `engine-crawlers.json`: `[{ engine, token, covers, appliesWhen, docUrl, checkedOn }]` with `covers` one of `images`, `favicons`, `video`, `news`, `shopping` and `appliesWhen` `always`, `news.enabled` or `feature:<name>` (a `search-engines.features` entry). Shipped entries: google `Googlebot-Image` (`images`, `favicons`; always), `Googlebot-Video` (`video`; `feature:video`), `Googlebot-News` (`news`; `news.enabled`), `Storebot-Google` (`shopping`; `feature:merchant-listing`); yandex `YandexImages` (`images`), `YandexFavicons` (`favicons`), `YandexVideo` (`video`); baidu `Baiduspider-image` (`images`), `Baiduspider-video` (`video`), `Baiduspider-news` (`news`). An entry applies when its engine is in `engines` ∪ {`google`} and its `appliesWhen` holds; tokens are taken from the vendor pages named in `docUrl` at ruleset build time.
  - Part `decision` (P0 · http): for every applicable entry, every URL of its `covers` set is allowed by the 04 § 15 robots.txt evaluation for that token on the robots.txt of the URL's own host (service origins here; resource hosts: SE-011). Sets, over the HTTP sample (04 § 10) of indexable pages: `images` — every `<img src|srcset>`, `<picture><source srcset>`, `og:image`, `twitter:image`, and JSON-LD `image`, `logo` and `thumbnailUrl` URL; `favicons` — every `link[rel~=icon]` and `link[rel=apple-touch-icon]` `href` plus `/favicon.ico` when no icon link exists; `video` — every `<video src>`, `<source src>` inside `<video>`, and JSON-LD `contentUrl`, `thumbnailUrl` and `video:content_loc`/`video:thumbnail_loc` sitemap URL; `news` — every indexable page with a `NewsArticle` node and every `<loc>` of a sitemap declaring the `news:` namespace; `shopping` — every indexable page with a `Product` node that has `offers`, its image URLs, and (with `merchantFeed`) every feed item `link` and `g:image_link`. A URL on a host that is not a service origin is judged by SE-011. Failure class `access-denied-wrongly` (URL, token, matching line, or "no group; `*` line N").
  - Part `unsupported` (P1 · http): a line of a robots.txt group whose field is `noindex`, `nofollow`, `noarchive` or `nosnippet` (case-insensitive) is a finding (`forbidden-value`: file, line, field): Google reads only `user-agent`, `allow`, `disallow` and `sitemap`, so such a line leaves its URLs indexable. Any other field name that is not `user-agent`, `allow`, `disallow`, `sitemap`, `crawl-delay`, `clean-param`, `host` or `content-signal` is a W (`pattern-match`).
  - Part `crawl-delay` (W · http): the effective group for `Bingbot` (04 § 15) has `Crawl-delay: d` with d seconds and a full pass over the site's URLs would take more than 7 days at one request per d seconds, i.e. `d × N > 604,800` where N is the number of distinct `<loc>` URLs in the sitemaps (`budget-exceeded`: d, N, days). Google ignores `crawl-delay`; Yandex dropped it; this part concerns Bing and the engines on its index.
  - Missing-input outcomes: no robots.txt (404) → parts `decision` and `crawl-delay` pass (SE-001 `missing` owns the W); robots.txt not 200 or unparsable → `skipped(robots-unavailable)` (SE-001 owns it); no applicable entry → `notApplicable`.
  - Sources: Google robots.txt specification; Google *Common crawlers*; Google *Favicon in Search*; Bing Webmaster Blog (crawl-delay).
  - Edits elsewhere: SE-002 gains "Auxiliary tokens: SE-010." at its end; the WCF coverage map (web-delivery § 2) lists SE-010 under *robots.txt & AI crawlers*.
  - Ownership rows: `SE-010 * WP-411 explicit`; ruleset `engine-crawlers.json` in the WP-104 data list (shape test: every entry has `docUrl` and `checkedOn`).

**6 — search-engines · SE-011 (Should)**, insert after SE-010.

- **SE-011** · P1 (part `health` W) · edge, http — Robots.txt of first-party resource hosts (robots.txt is per host, protocol and port). A **resource host** is a concrete host (a `*.` entry is resolved to the hosts actually referenced) of the `hosts` registry (03 § 3.3) with purpose `assets` that serves at least one resource URL referenced by the raw HTML of the HTTP sample (the SE-002 (b) reference forms, plus `og:image`, JSON-LD `image`/`logo`/`thumbnailUrl` and `link[rel~=icon]`). Part `access` (P1): the resource host's `/robots.txt` (fetched in the edge pass with `edge.production: true` from the production host; locally only when the host is served by a verify-environment service or host emulation, else `skipped(external-host)`) allows each such URL for `Googlebot` (scripts, stylesheets, fonts), `Googlebot-Image` (images, favicons) and `Bingbot` and for every other token SE-002 and SE-010 apply (`access-denied-wrongly`: URL, token, matching line). Part `health` (W): that robots.txt answers 5xx or 429 (Google stops crawling the host for the first 12 hours of such errors) (`status-mismatch`).
  - Missing-input outcomes: no resource host → `notApplicable`; a registry host that no sampled page references → not evaluated.
  - Ownership rows: `SE-011 * WP-411 explicit`, `SE-011 channel:edge WP-414 explicit`.

**2 — search-engines · SE-129 (Must)**, insert after SE-128 in § 3.2.

- **SE-129** · P0 (part `source-hint` W, part `dialog` W) · browser, ast — No back-button hijacking (Google spam policy, *Malicious practices*, added 2026-04-13: a site "interferes with user browser navigation by manipulating the browser history or other functionalities, preventing them from using their back button to immediately get back to the page they came from"). For each browser-sampled indexable page P (04 § 10) in a fresh browser context at the `mobile` viewport: let A be the page from which the crawl first reached P (the crawl graph's first discovery source, 04 § 10) when A is a `public` page; P is reached by clicking the plain `a[href]` to it on A (when that link is absent from A's rendered DOM, by `goto` P with `Referer` A); with no A (the homepage), the previous entry is `about:blank`. `h0` is `history.length` before the navigation. After `load` + 5 s, with no click, key or scroll input, `h1` is `history.length`; then `history.back()` once.
  - Part `entries` (P0 · browser): `h1 − h0 ≤ 1` (the navigation to P is one entry; any further entry added without user input is a finding, `forbidden-value`: URL, `h1 − h0`, the history API call and script URL when the browser records them).
  - Part `back` (P0 · browser): within 3 s after `history.back()` the document URL (04 § 10 normalisation, fragment ignored) equals A's URL (or `about:blank`); staying on P, moving to another URL, or being returned to P within 3 s (a forward re-navigation) is a finding (`value-mismatch`: expected, actual, script URL when recorded).
  - Part `dialog` (W · browser): a `beforeunload` dialog (Playwright `dialog` event of type `beforeunload`) opens during the back navigation (`forbidden-value`).
  - Part `source-hint` (W · ast): in files of role route, layout or component (04 § 7.1), a call to `history.pushState`, `history.go`, `history.forward`, `location.assign`, `location.replace` or an assignment to `location.href` inside a `popstate`, `pagehide` or `beforeunload` listener, or a `history.pushState` call outside any event-handler function at module or mount level (`useEffect`/`onMounted`/`DOMContentLoaded` bodies are mount level) whose URL argument is the current URL (`pattern-match`: call, listener). Adapters without a `historyApis` ApiRef list (03 § 6, entries `pushState`, `replaceState`, `go`, `forward`, `back`) → `noAdapterSupport` for this part.
  - Missing-input outcomes: no browser → `skipped(no-browser)`; `app` surfaces and pages behind `auth` → `notApplicable` (they are not indexable pages); router-initiated `replaceState` (no new entry) never counts.
  - Ownership rows: `SE-129 * WP-608 explicit`, `SE-129 part:source-hint WP-411 explicit`. New adapter manifest key `historyApis` (03 § 6; optional).

**3 — web-delivery · I18-011 (Should)**, insert after I18-010 in § 9; add `geoRestrictions` to the § 1 policy block.

- Policy: `web-delivery.geoRestrictions` (array of ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes, default `[]`): countries the Operator has chosen to block or redirect for legal reasons (sanctions, licensing). WCF-004 validates the codes. Ruleset `geo-headers.json`: `{ headers: [string], countries: [string], packages: [string] }` (shipped: headers `cf-ipcountry`, `cloudfront-viewer-country`, `x-vercel-ip-country`, `x-appengine-country`, `x-country-code`; countries `US GB DE FR JP IN BR AU`; packages `geoip-lite`, `maxmind`, `@maxmind/geoip2-node`, `ip-api`, `ipinfo`).
- **I18-011** · P1 (part `source` W) · http, ast — No IP-country-dependent serving to crawlers (Google: "Don't use IP analysis to adapt your content"; Googlebot crawls mostly from US addresses with no `Accept-Language`; I18-005 owns the `Accept-Language` case). Part `geo` (P1 · http): on the first 10 indexable pages of the HTTP sample (04 § 10, sample order), the page is requested with the Googlebot smartphone user agent and, in turn, with every header of `geo-headers.json` `headers` set to every code of `countries` (the front proxy, 04 § 3, sends the header itself; one request per code with all headers set to that code, so ≤ 8 extra requests per page), countries in `geoRestrictions` excluded. Each response must equal the response to the same request without the headers in status, `Location`, content type, `<title>`, meta description, robots directives (meta and `X-Robots-Tag`), canonical, hreflang pairs and generic `<main>` skeleton hash (as RTE-010 `behaviour`). A difference is a finding (`status-mismatch` for status or `Location`, `skeleton-mismatch` or `value-mismatch` otherwise: country, header, field, both values). A response of 403 or 451 for a country in `geoRestrictions` is expected and is not compared. Part `source` (W · ast): in files of role route, layout, middleware or component (04 § 7.1), a read of a header named in `geo-headers.json` `headers`, a call into a package of `geo-headers.json` `packages`, or an adapter `request.geo`-style member, whose result flows (04 § 13 data flow) into a redirect, rewrite, `notFound`/status ≥ 300, or a conditional that selects the rendering of `<main>` (`pattern-match`: read, sink).
  - Missing-input outcomes: `geoRestrictions` absent = `[]`; service host not `none` and edge-managed geo headers overwrite the probe's → the part runs in the local pass only, edge pass `notApplicable`.
  - Ownership rows: `I18-011 * WP-410 explicit` (family I18 in WP-410(c)); `geo-headers.json` in the WP-104 data list.
